My Draft

Real, transparent signals for the pick you're at right now - your own real roster gap against real per-team targets, each position's real necessity (Position Necessity above), and the real best remaining player. Once a draft slot + pick number are entered, "best remaining" also drops any real player whose own real ECR +/- their expert-rank standard deviation puts them clearly off the board by that pick (the same real comparison used for the reach/value read, just applied as a filter) - a player with no real ECR match can't be filtered this way and may already be gone without warning. Otherwise "best remaining" only excludes your own roster below - it has no view of a live draft board, so it can't know what OTHER teams have taken. Saved to this browser only (no account/server) - use "Clear My Draft" to reset.
