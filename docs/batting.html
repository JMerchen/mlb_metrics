How to play

  • Swing by clicking or tapping the spot where the ball crosses the plate, at the moment it gets there. With a mouse, the gold ring is your bat; the space bar swings wherever the ring is.
  • Timing decides where it goes: early pulls it, late pushes it the other way, and too far off either way is a foul or a miss. Aim decides how well you hit it: dead on is a line drive, a little under lifts it, over the top is a ground ball.
  • Don't swing at pitches outside the white box. Four balls is a walk; a pitch in the box you let go is a strike.
  • After each pitch a ring shows where it crossed (red for a strike, green for a ball, gold if you hit it) and a cross shows where your bat was.
  • Start at half speed. Real speed is what a hitter actually gets: about four tenths of a second.

What's real: each pitcher's pitch types, the speed of each, and how often he throws each one when he's ahead, behind or even in the count, to left- and right-handed batters (2026 season). What's typical: how each kind of pitch moves and where it's aimed is the same for every pitcher who throws it. What's made up: how a swing turns into a hit. It's a game, not one of our models.
