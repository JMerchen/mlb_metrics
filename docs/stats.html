Hitters

Three views of each hitter's season, each blending the full season with his last 81, 30 and 10 days so recent games count more. WAVE is his batting average (hits per at-bat), and hit chance his chance of at least one hit in 3.5 at-bats at that rate. CAVE (Carried Average) is the share of his games with at least one hit - a 1-for-4 and a 4-for-4 count the same. WOPS is his OPS. vs. pitchers faced credits hits off tough pitchers more and soft ones less, based on what every pitcher he faced allowed all season. Click a heading to sort.

Decision+ grades a hitter's swing-or-take choices: every pitch he saw is placed by where it crossed his strike zone, the count, outs, runners and pitch type, and scored by what his choice was worth over what the league usually does in that spot (league-average results, so it's the decision, not the contact). 100 is average; every 10 points is one standard deviation.
