Hitters

WAVE is a hitter's at-bat hit rate, with recent games counting more. Hit chance is his chance of at least one hit in 3.5 at-bats at that rate. Games with a hit counts his last 10 games with at least one hit (a 1-for-4 and a 4-for-4 count the same). WOPS is our gradient-boosted expected OPS for his next game, from his recent form and the starting pitcher he faces. Click a heading to sort.
