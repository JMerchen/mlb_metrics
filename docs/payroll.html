Payroll value

Winning isn't the only measure of success - winning for the money is. Each team's season is measured against its own league year, so a 1990 payroll and a 2026 payroll compare fairly. Payrolls are opening-day payrolls from The Baseball Cube. Payroll effectiveness above 1 means a team bought more wins per dollar than the league did; expected wins are what the seasons that spent most like it won.
