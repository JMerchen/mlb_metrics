Team rankings

Rating is how many points better than an average Division I team a team is over 100 possessions (about a game and a half), after adjusting for who it has played and where. It splits into offense (points scored above average) and defense (points allowed below average), each with its national rank. Early in the season the ratings lean on last season's; by January this season's games carry them.
